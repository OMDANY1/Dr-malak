# Article Detail

URLs: /ar/articles/[slug] and /en/articles/[slug].

Implement page.tsx beside this specification.

Server-rendered article page with breadcrumb navigation, title, summary, relevant cover image, accurate author attribution, publication/update dates only when real, calculated reading time, table of contents generated from headings with anchor links, related published articles, and consultation CTA. Returns 404 for missing or unpublished draft slugs.
