Approximate distribution: 75–85% ivory, 10–20% red, remainder neutral photography. This is a compositional guide, not a rigid formula.
Original board vocabulary: refined, natural, individualized, trusted, modern, feminine. Warm portraits, restrained fine geometry, visible natural skin texture, generous negative space. The board contains a red central composition; incorporate one meaningful red section rather than painting every panel red.
Recent Instagram screenshot also shows light fabric and gentle shadows. Those are optional supporting textures, not required imagery in every section.
No new logo, no wholesale brand change, no purple/mauve replacement. The small orange sparkle belongs to the reference logo; it does not introduce a gold website palette.
