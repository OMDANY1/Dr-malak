# Project brief
Client: د. ملاك السيف / Dr. Malak Alsaif.
Deliverable: working local Arabic-first bilingual personal medical website, no production deployment.
Audience: people researching plastic/reconstructive consultation in Saudi Arabia; tone warm, factual and respectful. Audience focus can be feminine without claiming the practice excludes men.
Primary journey: Instagram → understand doctor → explore broad areas → WhatsApp coordination.
Success: recognizable existing identity, readable mobile experience, trustworthy content, functioning contact links. No fabricated proof points.
Scope: five page types in two languages. No dashboard, CRM, database, booking engine, patient records, or payment gateway.
