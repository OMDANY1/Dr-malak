# Editorial rules
Use content/site-copy.json as the copy starting point and content/site-config.json for contact values. They are data, not an already implemented CMS.
Arabic is natural, clear and professionally warm. English must be idiomatic, not word-for-word. No lorem ipsum in visitor-facing UI.
Hero positioning expresses the consultation experience; it does not promise a clinical outcome.
Verified credentials may be paraphrased; do not exaggerate title, scope, or institution relationship.
Specialty descriptions should explain broad fields with no recovery times, risk percentages, specific treatment recommendations, or result guarantees. Keep wording brief and flag medical review in handoff.
Do not publish invented first-person quotes, testimonials, or placeholder ratings. Omit unpopulated content sections entirely.
All user-facing errors and labels must be translated. Internal pending-asset instructions belong in the handoff, not rendered website cards.
